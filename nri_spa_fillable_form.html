📋 Required Documents Checklist - Hyderabad/Telangana

  • Copy of Passport (All Executants) - Valid passport
  • Copy of PAN Card (All Executants and Attorney)
  • Copy of Aadhaar Card (All Executants and Attorney)
  • Copy of Attorney's ID Proof with current address
  • Sale Deed/Agreement to Sell (if already executed)
  • Seller's original title documents
  • Encumbrance Certificate (EC) for 13-30 years
  • Latest Property Tax receipt (GHMC/HMDA)
  • Layout Approval (if applicable)
  • Building Approval Plan (if constructed property)
  • Photographs of Executants and Attorney (passport size)
  • Power of Attorney (original + 2 copies)
  • Form 60/61 or Form 15CA/15CB (for tax purposes, if applicable)

🏢 Sub-Registrar Offices (SRO) in Hyderabad

Major SRO Locations:

  • SRO Khairatabad - For Banjara Hills, Jubilee Hills, Somajiguda areas
  • SRO Secunderabad - For Secunderabad, Trimulgherry, Marredpally areas
  • SRO L.B. Nagar - For LB Nagar, Vanasthalipuram, Nagole areas
  • SRO Kukatpally - For Kukatpally, KPHB, Miyapur areas
  • SRO Serilingampally - For Gachibowli, Madhapur, Hi-Tech City areas
  • SRO Rajendranagar - For Rajendranagar, Attapur, Shaikpet areas

Note: Registration must be done at the SRO having jurisdiction over the property location. Online slot booking is available on the Telangana Registration Department website.

⚠️ Important Notes - Telangana/Hyderabad Registration

  • This document must be executed before a Notary Public in the country of residence
  • The document must be apostilled or attested by Indian Embassy/Consulate
  • Original Power of Attorney must be presented at the Sub-Registrar Office in Hyderabad/Telangana
  • Stamp duty in Telangana: 4% for men, 3% for women (as per latest rates, verify current rates)
  • Registration fee: 0.5% of property value (maximum capped as per Telangana Registration Act)
  • Both principals must sign in presence of two witnesses
  • Attorney must accept the appointment by signing the acceptance clause
  • For GHMC properties: Ensure property tax is paid up to date before registration
  • Obtain Encumbrance Certificate (EC) for the last 13-30 years from SRO
  • Mutation in Dharani portal is mandatory after registration
  • HMDA/GHMC approval may be required for certain properties

Special Powers Granted

1. REGISTRATION & DOCUMENTATION:

  • To represent us before the Registration Department, Telangana
  • To attend before the concerned Sub-Registrar of Assurances or any other Registration Office in India
  • To execute, sign, verify and register the Sale Deed/Conveyance Deed/Transfer Deed and all other documents relating to the purchase and registration of the above-mentioned property
  • To submit applications, affidavits, declarations, and other documents
  • To verify, admit execution and get registered all documents relating to the said property
  • To obtain certified copies, ESCs, receipts, and other documents
  • To pay stamp duty, registration fees, and all other charges, taxes, and dues payable to the Government or any authority

2. FINANCIAL TRANSACTIONS:

  • To make payment of the sale consideration to the seller(s) by any banking mode
  • To receive possession of the property and execute necessary documents
  • To open, operate, and close bank accounts for this transaction
  • To obtain and submit necessary tax forms including Form 15CA/15CB if applicable

3. VERIFICATION & CLEARANCES:

  • To verify and obtain encumbrance certificate, title deeds, and approved building plans
  • To obtain property tax receipts, utility bills and clearances
  • To conduct legal due diligence on the property
  • To verify ownership, title, and all related matters
  • To obtain NOCs from any authority if required

4. MUTATION & POST-REGISTRATION:

  • To apply for and obtain mutation of the property in revenue records (Dharani Portal)
  • To update ownership in GHMC/HMDA records and obtain new property tax assessment
  • To apply for conversion of land use if required under Telangana Land Revenue Act
  • To obtain Encumbrance Certificate (EC) from Sub-Registrar Office
  • To transfer property records, pay any revision or registration fees

5. TELANGANA-SPECIFIC AUTHORITIES:

  • To appear before GHMC, HMDA, Mandal Revenue Office (MRO), or Tahsildar's Office
  • To apply for and obtain Layout Approval (if applicable under GHMC/HMDA)
  • To pay and obtain receipts for Property Tax, Water Tax, Betterment Charges
  • To update records in Dharani (Integrated Land Records Management System)

6. REPRESENTATION:

  • To appear before any Government Department, Municipal Corporation, Gram Panchayat, Revenue authorities, or any other authority
  • To sign all applications, affidavits, undertakings, and declarations
  • To engage advocates, surveyors, valuers and other professionals
  • To do all acts which we could have done if personally present
  • To answer interrogatories and give such replies as may be called upon to be given
